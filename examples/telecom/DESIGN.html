Decision brief

Page 1 of the report. Everything below is a proposal built from one brief.

Help customers understand a telecom complaint and receive a verified service credit.

Start with verified outage credits. Investigate speed and billing complaints, then send unclear cases to staff. Keep pending cases owned.

Key design calls

  1. 1
    Code sets credit amounts.
    Rejected: Model arithmetic. The same facts must yield the same credit.
  2. 2
    Staff approve unclear or larger credits.
    Rejected: Open model authority. Exceptions need a named owner.
  3. 3
    Check uncertain payments before retry.
    Rejected: Blind second attempt. Avoid paying the same incident twice.
  4. 4
    Show progress in the customer portal.
    Rejected: Unowned background waits. Customers see who owns the next step.

How we will know it works

Measure durable complaint resolutionsee definitionSeven-day reopen rate = complaints reopened within7d / complaints receiving a decision, by decision week. Financial correctness = wrong/duplicate/unsupported credit count and value per posted credit; safety counts are never averaged away. Collect two-week manual baseline before setting effect targets.

Brief and scope

The brief as given, and how this design reads it. Facts the brief did not give are assumptions with owners.

Brief (verbatim)
Design a complaint assistant for a telecom provider. Customers report network outages, poor speed and billing errors. The assistant should investigate using account, billing and service records, explain findings and resolve eligible service credits. Larger or unclear refunds need a human supervisor and may wait up to 48 hours. Keep customers informed, avoid duplicate refunds and use only the right customer's active service connection. Propose any missing policies as assumptions, not real company rules.
FieldDesign reading
Scope First releaseStart with verified outage credits. Investigate speed and billing complaints, then send unclear cases to staff. Keep pending cases owned.
CTX1 Decision and boundaryA verified customer opens a complaint in an authenticated portal. Investigate outages, poor speed and billing errors. Automatically post only eligible service credits. Supervisors decide larger or unclear credits; external cash refunds, service changes, outage repair and disputed invoice correction remain owned manual work.
CTX2 End conditions and valueSuccess is an evidence-linked explanation plus proven credit or explicit no-credit decision. Missing records, authentication failure, denial, cancellation, overdue and unknown posting remain distinct owned outcomes. Never label pending money as resolved.
CTX3 Evidence limitsThis is a proposed design with synthetic fixtures. No company policy, adapter behavior, staffing, savings or regulatory compliance has been verified. H1-H4 block live launch.
