## Proposed assumption ledger

### H1

Proposed first release ingests text typed or pasted by an operator, plus optional asset ID/address; direct citizen-channel integration, photo interpretation and ticket closure are deferred.

Why: A usable bounded release requires no invented channel contract.

Proposed owner: City service desk lead

Validation: Confirm intake channels and observe a shift; agree excluded formats.

Launch blocker: true

### H2

A city-scoped ticket ledger, GIS asset registry and versioned crew routing table can provide read APIs; create-ticket and attach-report commands require a verified adapter.

Why: The brief names no actual systems or available APIs.

Proposed owner: City IT integration lead

Validation: Obtain sandbox schemas and demonstrate reads, conditional writes, durable idempotency lookup and report uniqueness. If unavailable run read-only assist only.

Launch blocker: true

### H3

Each ticket write requires a currently authorized operator approval; tenant is city ID, and operation, resource, report revision, immutable payload digest and policy version are bound to approval.

Why: Unknown city authority defaults to no autonomous write.

Proposed owner: City service owner and IT security lead

Validation: Approve role matrix, routing authority and API-side checks; test revocation and cross-city denial.

Launch blocker: true

### H4

Potential exposed wires, fallen poles, sparks or fire enter a staffed urgent manual queue immediately; proposed acknowledgement 2 elapsed minutes and supervisor escalation at 2 minutes; routine exceptions acknowledge within 30 staffed minutes and escalate at 30.

Why: Safety routing and real out-of-hours coverage must be agreed locally; these are proposed timers, not emergency advice.

Proposed owner: City duty supervisor

Validation: Sign off local emergency procedure, staffed calendar, fallback contact and coverage; drill overdue/out-of-hours delivery.

Launch blocker: true

### H5

Suggested duplicates require operator selection of an existing ticket on the same asset; fuzzy similarity never merges or suppresses reports. Existing ticket links do not change crew assignment or close work.

Why: Different faults or repaired/reopened work can resemble one another.

Proposed owner: Maintenance operations lead

Validation: Review same-asset recurrence and multi-fault examples; verify link behavior in sandbox.

Launch blocker: true

### H6

Proposed data policy: redact reporter contact before LLM use; raw local intake expires after 30 days, recommendation snapshots after 90 days, minimal audit receipts after 365 days; residency and legal retention require city approval.

Why: The brief supplies no privacy or records policy.

Proposed owner: City data protection/records owner

Validation: Approve retention/residency and deletion exceptions before real records; validate deletes and audit integrity.

Launch blocker: true

### H7

Proposed routing uses a versioned GIS boundary, asset ownership, fault category and crew capability/on-duty table; unresolved boundary, unavailable crew or conflicting asset evidence goes to supervisor.

Why: No crew jurisdiction or roster is supplied.

Proposed owner: Maintenance operations lead

Validation: Publish authoritative policy version and validate boundary/roster samples with crews.

Launch blocker: true

### H8

Illustrative planning: 90% of reports reach the single LLM component, 5% of those need one format/transient retry, 2000 input and 300 output tokens/call, USD 1/input-million and 4/output-million; provider and actual prices remain unselected.

Why: Budget must be reproducible without invented provider facts.

Proposed owner: Product owner and IT procurement

Validation: Measure branch mix, context length, latency and quoted prices on approved provider. No spend authorized.

Launch blocker: false

### H9

Proposed gates: zero unauthorized/wrong-city/equipment operations and zero duplicate records in synthetic controls; urgent recall at least 99% on adjudicated offline set; routine routing agreement at least 95%; active handling-time reduction target 20% relative to baseline.

Why: Thresholds are hypotheses for review, not measured results or accepted residual risk.

Proposed owner: City service owner

Validation: Approve thresholds and powered evaluation before pilot; no safety breach can be averaged away.

Launch blocker: true

### H10

Proposed operational limits: each read attempt 5 seconds, one read retry; LLM attempt 10 seconds with one retry; ticket write timeout 5 seconds with zero automatic write retry; reconciliation reads at 5, 30 and 120 seconds, then owned unknown hold with 15-minute read-only watchdog. Proposed additional limits: snapshot age at plan creation 60 seconds, plan/approval expiry 5 elapsed minutes, routine nonhuman response p95 30 seconds, and unknown-held >1 hour escalates service manager. These limits require verification, not established policy.

Why: Bounded behavior and human ownership are required for implementability.

Proposed owner: City IT operations lead

Validation: Verify latency/capacity, idempotency retention and unknown-commit support in sandbox.

Launch blocker: true

### H11

Proposed measurement uses two representative working weeks of baseline/shadow and two working weeks of randomized pilot, extending to the power-analysis requirement; no sample size or savings are established. Proposed assist usability phase lasts one working week; live pilot is one approved crew area and remains operator-confirmed.

Why: 200 reports/day is volume, not evidence of statistical power.

Proposed owner: Service improvement analyst

Validation: Estimate handling-time variance, within-operator correlation, strata prevalence and cluster-adjusted sample size.

Launch blocker: false
