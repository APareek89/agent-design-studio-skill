## Honesty and protocol limits

- 101 actual tests pass with zero skips, including all 58 original tests unchanged. See [final test log](final-tests.log). Planned business assertions were not executed.
- Final Telecom/Health are feedback-refined, independently re-reviewed outputs. Fresh repeats exposed real failures preserved in iterations/04 and /05. There are no two consecutive all-target passes and no untouched transfer estimate.
- Separate fresh authors were run for baseline and iterations01/03. Intermediate repairs/rerenders did not all receive fresh author sessions.
- Original flawed seed files were never altered. Active corrected seeds are versioned in seeded-defects/v2 with historical SHA256s and exact construction/mutation provenance.
- Civic/CSV retain legacy schema1.0 and honest self-review; their new visual grading is independent. New draft reviews are independent; one reviewer disclosed incidental exposure to validation.json after forming initial findings. The skill now explicitly withholds embedded author reviews for the first independent pass.
- Human authority, real adapters, policy correctness, privacy approval and measured benefit remain launch dependencies. No business/provider calls or deployment were performed.
