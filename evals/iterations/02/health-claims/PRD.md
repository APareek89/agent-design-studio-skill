# Inpatient claim review assistant

> PASS | DESIGN ONLY. self-review; round 3.

## Original brief

Design a health insurance claim adjudication assistant for inpatient hospital claims. It should read submitted policy and hospital documents, assess coverage and eligible charges, recommend or process eligible claims, and route exceptions and rejections for review. Policies can change over time and bills may include room-rent limits. Protect personal medical data, explain decisions with evidence and propose any missing rules as assumptions, not verified insurance or medical advice.


## Context

- **CTX1 Review inpatient claims**: For insurer claims staff: authenticated inpatient submission starts a run; a stored review packet with assigned queue ends it. Documents inform evidence-linked coverage and charge recommendations. Staff alone adjudicate, reject and pay. Outpatient, medical diagnosis, fraud verdicts, appeals and automated final decisions are excluded. A wrong-tenant request ends before protected content is read. Missing documents create an owned pending review packet with no amount.
- **CTX2 Policy and bill authority**: Authoritative insurer policy registry selects the contract and applicable endorsements at the policy-defined event date, not the newest upload. Conflicting or missing rules mean needs_review. Human-verified invoice facts, authenticated member IDs and code-selected policy version alone supply amounts and dates. Model extraction is provisional; it cannot turn an unverified field into a bill fact. Every rule result links rule ID, version and source page.
- **CTX3 Record and safety targets**: Target: reduce reviewer touch time while preserving zero unauthorized final decisions and exact rule arithmetic on the approved suite. Targets are hypotheses. This design has no live integration, regulatory opinion, medical advice, measured accuracy or stakeholder sign-off. Source content may contain hostile instructions. Final report says ready for review, never claim approved.

## Tasks

- **T0 Prepare a claim for review**: Run T1 through T5 in order; unsafe intake stops. Later missing facts route a pending packet, not a fabricated amount.
- **T1 Check access and collect evidence**: Authenticate tenant and member before reading. Fail closed on identity or access conflict.
- **T2 Read and cite documents**: Summarize coverage evidence; abstain on missing, contradictory or unreadable pages.
- **T3 Apply approved policy rules**: Code checks versioned rules against verified facts. Outcome is recommended_eligible, rejection_candidate or needs_review; ordered precedence: missing/conflict, exclusion, then eligible.
- **T4 Save and assign the review**: Fresh authorization and immutable claim/rule versions must pass before conditional insert; reconcile unknown result.
- **T5 Confirm the handoff**: Show record ID and assigned queue only after readback. Pending or blocked stays visibly pending.
- **A1 Identity and authority**: Load trusted attributes from Authenticated insurer identity, tenant/member registry and role revocation version.; model proposals are separate.
- **A2 Policy and hospital evidence**: Load trusted attributes from Immutable policy registry, endorsements and document page hashes. Bill facts require staff verification distinct from model output.; model proposals are separate.
- **A3 Freshness and queue**: Load trusted attributes from Claim version, operation key, packet record, reviewer assignment and insurer business calendar.; model proposals are separate.

## Components

- **C1 Trusted case reader**: Resolve session tenant before fetching claim; enforce row-level member scope and document ACLs; 10s timeout, one read retry; unavailable creates no external write. Uploaded policy is evidence only, not authority. Schema rejects cross-subject hospital pages. Malware scan, page limits and redaction precede model access. Extract only visible rendered-page pixels through sandboxed OCR; discard PDF hidden/invisible text, off-page text, scripts, attachments, comments and nonvisible form layers. Preserve visibly rendered form content as page evidence. Create immutable document/page IDs plus content hash for normalized visible text (Unicode NFC; CRLF to LF only). Never concatenate raw PDF text layers into prompts. Model sees visible text only, with page IDs. Visible document instructions remain untrusted data. OCR ambiguity or an OCR/source discrepancy sets a review flag; staff verifies facts before calculation. textHash is SHA-256 of normalized visibleText encoded in UTF-8, computed by trusted store.
- **C2 Evidence reader**: Reads untrusted policy and hospital documents as data. Produces no authenticated identities, amounts or effective dates for writes. Prompt injections are quoted as suspicious content; never followed. 30s timeout; malformed or unavailable output gets one retry then a fixed abstention narrative. No diagnosis or medical necessity ruling. Citation format includes document/page IDs, exact start/end offsets and verbatim quote; policy citations require approved clause ID. C3 independently verifies every citation before any packet can be saved. A model-provided page hash or clause label is never trusted.
- **C3 Rule and packet validator**: Approved rule interpreter uses integer minor units, policy-specific rounding order and effective event-date rules. Never infer room-rent linked deductions: explicit charge class rule required, otherwise needs_review. Apply sublimits, exclusions, deductible, copay and remaining sum insured in signed policy order. Missing/ambiguous facts => needs_review amount absent. Missing active policy edition => needs_review. Immediately re-read claim/rule/authority before write; stale or revoked fails without write. No model fact authorizes money. Rules include effective date and timezone boundaries; document amendments do not overwrite historical editions. Citation guard runs first on all C2 evidence: resolve document/page only in C1 authorized immutable corpus; require source hash from store, 0<=start<end<=len(visibleText), and exact Unicode-code-point slice equal to quote. Policy clauses must exist in the selected registry edition, be applicable to this event, and contain the full cited span. Every narrative claim must reference at least one verified evidence ID; render only a template from claim/evidence pairs, not unchecked free prose. Nonexistent page, mismatched quote, wrong/inapplicable clause or unbound narrative claim => status invalid, precise reason, no packet write, Claims lead owns blocked resubmission. Fixed code abstention uses no citations. These checks establish source correspondence, not that a medical assertion is true. For proposed PR1 only: verified roomDailyMinor>0, roomDays a policy-approved positive integer, cap>=0; room eligible=min(actualDaily,cap)*days. For each preeligible linked charge line with class in prorateClasses, lineEligible=floor(lineMinor*min(actualDaily,cap)/actualDaily). Unlisted/explicitly excluded linked classes retain full preeligible amount. Apply exactly once per line; do not prorate room line itself, nonpayable lines or the aggregate again. Sum room+eligible linked/unlinked lines; subtract deductible with floor at zero, apply floor(sum*(10000-copayBps)/10000), then remaining-sum-insured cap. Missing/unknown proration mode, duplicate class mapping, actualDaily=0, currency mismatch, invalid days or missing approved class membership => needs_review with no amount. A nonempty class list without approved formulaId PR1 and linked classes is insufficient. PR1 is hypothetical; H2 must verify the real insurer rule.
- **C4 Review packet store**: Proposed adapter POST review-packets atomically inserts immutable packet and assignment, notifies staff through the existing queue, and stores operation key under a unique constraint. No payment or final-adjudication capability. Server rechecks authority, claimVersion and ruleVersion inside the transaction; 10s timeout. Exact duplicate returns existing record after body equality; changed intent conflicts. No write retry after timeout. Readback is required even on success. Reject PACKET_BODY_MISMATCH as invalid before insert when any proposed body differs from immutable C3 packet, including amount, class-derived result, narrative bytes or cited evidence.
- **C5 Readback and reconciliation**: GET packet by operation key under tenant-scoped recovery service; compares full body and assigned reviewer, not just status. Read every 10s up to three times. Signed callbacks deduplicated by record ID and event sequence; expired caller session does not remove service recovery authority. Ambiguous commit freezes changed intent and is owned by Platform on-call until lookup resolves. Never insert during recovery. absent/pending/unavailable after bound => unresolved operation retained for manual reconciliation, no completion. Cancellation before insert cancels run; after insert or unknown result records operator intent in operations ledger and cannot revoke a clinical claim decision, since none exists.
- **C6 Record proof check**: Require nonempty durable packet record ID, matching tenant/submission/body versions and actual reviewer assignment. A provisional local ID or model phrase cannot prove completion. Existing queue owns acknowledgement and overdue alerts under H5; no person approval is claimed by mere assignment.
- **C7 Read-only status view**: Renders session UI only; no messages or external writes. completed_review_packet means ready for review, not claim adjudicated. Pending recovery points to operation key and on-call; access denied discloses no member data. Reviewer queue sends existing-system notices under H5, requiring verification before launch.

## Framework

- **F1 Code owns the workflow**: Durable code state machine plus one bounded document reader A single evidence reader fits judgement; persistence, rule maths and recovery remain code.
- **F2 Keep long-lived state durable**: Database-backed worker with transactional outbox and versioned run state Compare state machine and durable workflow engine on recovery, version migrations, audit and operations; choose code worker for one bounded pipeline. No current vendor capability is asserted.
- **F3 Limit service permissions**: Reader can read one authorized claim; writer can create one draft packet; recovery can only read operation status Fresh role/version check at server write; model has no credentials; assignment is provisional and does not approve claim.
- **F4 Keep medical records separate**: Regional encrypted document store plus relational authoritative policy/claim ledger; retrieval index contains redacted citations only Privacy owner approves access/region/retention. Encrypt with scoped keys; audit record IDs and access actors without medical text. Delete model cache immediately; proposed retention 90 days with approved legal-hold exceptions; tombstones propagate to derived indexes and backups per approved schedule.

## Models

- **M1 Bounded document reading**: Standard tier is an unverified starting hypothesis for long medical-document citations. Small tier rejected until it matches citation/abstention accuracy; reasoning tier rejected for latency/cost unless held-out errors justify it. W2 is the sole invocation: 1 call + H6 5% retry = 1.05 expected, maximum 2 calls; C1 rejection may lower actual average. 12000 input and 1500 output tokens/call: 1.05*(12000*2+1500*8)/1e6 = USD 0.0378/case, USD 37.80 per 1000 cases. Worst case USD 0.072 and 60s model time, up to about 122s whole run with I/O retries/readback. Prices are illustrative, not quoted; exclude OCR, hosting, storage, retrieval, human review and taxes. More than 12000 redacted tokens abstains and routes manual; no hidden chunk calls.

## Rollout

- **R1 Reduce reviewer effort**: Baseline then assist. No improvement claim until powered comparison completes.
- **R2 Protect claimants**: All phases. Zero safety incidents; separate strata quality review.
- **R3 Shadow before staff use**: Shadow: proposed 2 weeks minimum. Entry: H2/H4 approved test use and deidentified fixtures. Exit: EV1-EV18 run in harness; SME disagreement resolved; adapter spike H3 complete.
- **R4 Assist with draft packets**: Assist: proposed 2 weeks minimum. Entry: H1-H5 signed and EV20 passes; trained reviewers. Exit: all assignments owned, no safety incident, representative queue SLA evidence and complete metrics. Stop: disable inserts; retain readback/recovery.
- **R5 Bounded pilot then scale assist**: Pilot: randomized assist-only pilot for >=4 weeks after power sizing; scale remains assist-only.. Entry: assist exit, approved sample/power plan and owner authorization. Exit: powered R1 benefit, no safety breach, no worsened audited decision rate and H5 capacity. Scale after Claims lead decision; autonomy expansion needs new design.
- **R6 Compare reviewer effort fairly**: Pilot. Collect baseline variance and intrareviewer correlation, power a two-sided mean comparison at proposed alpha .05/power .8; adjust reviewer-cluster design effect and attrition. Unknown variance prevents sample claim.
- **R7 Own failures and recovery**: All phases. Signed runbooks, writer kill switch drill, pending-operation recovery drill and queue coverage before launch; only insurer executives may accept residual risk. No acceptance recorded here.
