# Telecom complaint and service credit assistant

> NEEDS-WORK | DESIGN ONLY. independent-agent; round 1.

## Original brief

Design a complaint assistant for a telecom provider. Customers report network outages, poor speed and billing errors. The assistant should investigate using account, billing and service records, explain findings and resolve eligible service credits. Larger or unclear refunds need a human supervisor and may wait up to 48 hours. Keep customers informed, avoid duplicate refunds and use only the right customer's active service connection. Propose any missing policies as assumptions, not real company rules.


## Context

- **CTX1 Decision and boundary**: A verified customer opens a complaint in an authenticated portal. Investigate outages, poor speed and billing errors. Automatically post only eligible service credits. Supervisors decide larger or unclear credits; external cash refunds, service changes, outage repair and disputed invoice correction remain owned manual work.
- **CTX2 End conditions and value**: Success is an evidence-linked explanation plus proven credit or explicit no-credit decision. Missing records, authentication failure, denial, cancellation, overdue and unknown posting remain distinct owned outcomes. Never label pending money as resolved.
- **CTX3 Evidence limits**: This is a proposed design with synthetic fixtures. No company policy, adapter behavior, staffing, savings or regulatory compliance has been verified. H1-H4 block live launch.

## Tasks

- **T0 Resolve the complaint**: Run T1 then T2 then choose T3 outcome then T4 closure.
- **T1 Check customer and connection**: Authenticate tenant/customer, explicitly select one active connection; never default across multiple services.
- **T2 Investigate and explain**: Read account, bills, service incidents; show cited findings or missing/conflicting evidence.
- **T3 Choose a credit route**: Code partitions: eligible under cap; larger/unclear; proved no-credit. Missing data uses larger/unclear route.
- **T3.1 Post an eligible service credit**: Code policy or exact signed supervisor approval plus fresh checks authorizes one credit.
- **T3.2 Handle supervisor review**: Queue, acknowledgement, 24h update and 48h decision or overdue ownership; deny/cancel prevents new money.
- **T3.3 Explain a no-credit result**: A proved zero entitlement or unsupported external refund becomes an explained decision/manual handoff.
- **T4 Prove outcome and retain ownership**: Verify posting or durable case proof; unknown posts retain owner and frozen event key.
- **A1 Customer and selected active connection**: Authenticated identity gateway and service registry; tenantId, customerId, connectionId, active flag, connectionVersion, authRevocationVersion.
- **A2 Evidence and policy**: Versioned billing invoices, incident telemetry, complaint and approved policy store; observedAt, period start/end UTC, timezone, paid recurring amount, currency, overlap/event IDs.
- **A3 Financial and approval state**: Authoritative credit ledger businessEventKey, operationId, postingId; immutable approval planId/version and approver identity; durable queue/timer events.

## Framework

- **F1 A durable code workflow**: Use one durable code state machine with a bounded explanation model. Code owns route/amount/clock. Planner adds authority risk; request handler cannot own 48h waits. Compare database-backed workers and workflow engine on durable timers, event ordering, retry visibility and operational burden; start database worker plus transactional outbox; choose a workflow engine if replay/recovery support is easier for team.
- **F2 State and evidence ownership**: Relational case store for scope/versions/queue/event keys; immutable approved plans and append-only audit. Billing ledger remains financial truth. Retrieval may help explanation later, but cannot decide entitlement. Inbox events unique by provider eventId; DB compare-and-swap by caseVersion. Outbox and timers resume on worker restart. Local state updates are internal bookkeeping, never telecom account mutations. Portal GET exposes durable workflow notices without email/SMS side effects.
- **F3 Separate read and credit identities**: Gateway checks tenant/customer and active selected connection before reads; credit adapter only posts a scoped service-credit command. Small blast radius; readback service identity may outlive portal session. Every financial writer enforces fresh plan and event key; human approval cannot bypass source evidence or revoked authority. Secrets via deployment secret store, rotation and no model access.
- **F4 Data deployment candidates**: Choose managed relational DB, encrypted object audit and a secrets service in approved region; provider decision remains H4 spike. AWS: RDS/object storage/secrets candidates; GCP: Cloud SQL/object storage/secret manager candidates; Azure: managed PostgreSQL/blob/key vault candidates; open source: PostgreSQL/S3-compatible storage/Vault candidates. Compare retention, residency, access audit, restore and operator load on identical requirements. These are candidates; no current capability, pricing or compliance claim. Minimize model data to evidence extracts with pseudonymous IDs; never payment details.

## Rollout

- **R1 Measure durable complaint resolution**: Evidence citation precision supports explanation trust; route agreement reduces avoidable supervisor load; timer/notice lag protects customer follow-through. Cost and safety metrics are guardrails, not assumed causal gains.
- **R2 Shadow without customer effects**: Entry: synthetic EV23 passes and lawful scoped read approvals. Exit: two weeks representative labelled traces covering outage/speed/billing and active/multi-connection strata; EV21 thresholds with adjudicated disagreements; no scoped data breach.
- **R3 Assist staff with reviewed plans**: Entry: Shadow exit plus staff queue training and H1/H3 approvals. Exit: independently labelled plans meet EV21; H2 sandbox proves commit checks, uniqueness and readback; EV22 alarms exercised. No pilot effect required for entry.
- **R4 Bounded automatic-credit pilot**: Entry: Assist exit, H1-H4 signed and Billing owner enables scoped adapter. Exit: powered R6 observation complete; zero unsafe financial effects, EV21 quality holds in each stratum, no unowned overdue/unknown case. Product and Billing owners decide expansion.
- **R5 Expand only with evidence**: Entry: Pilot exit and owner decision. Weekly audit and EV23 on each policy/adapter change. Roll back new automatic traffic on any wrong-subject, duplicate or unproven completion; existing uncertain operations retain keys and reconciliation identity.
- **R6 Run a controlled complaint experiment**: Randomize authenticated customer account, sticky assignment, stratified by complaint type, language and connection count. Same household account never crosses arms; staff tool clearly labels control and cannot transfer cases. Manual process control vs bounded assistant treatment, same eligibility and supervisors. Observe at least four weekly cycles and7d follow-up, extending for planned sample. Compute two-proportion non-inferiority sample from baseline p, pre-agreed margin, alpha .05 and power .80; adjust repeated accounts by design effect1+(m-1)ICC; estimate ICC during baseline. For time outcome use baseline variance and continuous-outcome power separately. Fix margin/sample before examining treatment outcomes; safety stops immediate regardless of power.
- **R7 Own every pending case**: Support owns supervisor waits and1h acknowledgement/48h decision; duty manager owns overdue. Billing owns uncertain posting, hourly read-only recovery and ledger reconciliation. Security owns scope/revocation incidents; model owner owns unsupported explanations. Product can expand only with all required gate owners.
