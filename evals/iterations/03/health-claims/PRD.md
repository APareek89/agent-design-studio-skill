# Inpatient claim review assistant

> PASS | DESIGN ONLY. self-review; round 3.

## Original brief

Design a health insurance claim adjudication assistant for inpatient hospital claims. It should read submitted policy and hospital documents, assess coverage and eligible charges, recommend or process eligible claims, and route exceptions and rejections for review. Policies can change over time and bills may include room-rent limits. Protect personal medical data, explain decisions with evidence and propose any missing rules as assumptions, not verified insurance or medical advice.


## Context

- **CTX1 Business decision**: Help a claims examiner decide coverage and eligible charges from inpatient documents; produce a draft explanation and a recorded next step.
- **CTX2 Boundary**: Starts with an authenticated claim submission or provisional case. Ends with a proven staff-facing case record or an owned unresolved case. No payment, final rejection, clinical diagnosis, fraud judgement or appeal adjudication.
- **CTX3 Policy authority**: Use coverage applicable on service dates and endorsement precedence from the insurer rule registry. Current publication date alone cannot select the policy. Missing/contradictory policy routes to staff, never inferred rules.
- **CTX4 Data boundary**: Authenticated tenant/member access precedes document fetch. Only source-visible text and verified OCR enter model context. Documents and retrieval indexes do not become policy authority.

## Tasks

- **T0 Review an inpatient claim**: Run intake, evidence, interpretation, calculation, exception review, record and proof in order; conditional steps skip only by declared routes.
- **T1 Check claimant and scope**: Authorize tenant/member access; create provisional context from metadata when documents are missing.
- **T2 Read and verify documents**: Fetch authorized visible documents, verify OCR against page pixels, preserve page/clause anchors and detect conflicts.
- **T3 Draft evidence explanation**: Suggest supported coverage issues; abstain for ambiguity. Never set authoritative amounts or dates.
- **T4 Calculate eligible charges**: Code selects effective rule pack, computes minor-unit line eligibility, limits and remaining benefits; withhold totals for unknown rules.
- **T5 Resolve review and missing evidence**: Staff acknowledge, request evidence, review rejection/exception and choose an owned disposition; cancellation stops analysis.
- **T6 Record recommendation safely**: Persist staff-facing outcome under a fresh access/version check; reconcile uncertain saves before retry.
- **T7 Prove or own the result**: Show a proven saved case ID, or retain an unresolved operation with named owner and next deadline.
- **A1 Identity and authority**: Versioned read snapshots are distinct from model narrative.
- **A2 Evidence and policy**: Versioned read snapshots are distinct from model narrative.
- **A3 Review and save state**: Versioned read snapshots are distinct from model narrative.

## Components

- **C1 Authorize intake**: Identity, command, correction links and permission are enforced before any medical read. Corrections require originalCaseId and expectedVersion; they open a linked draft only, never change settlement.
- **C2 Verify source evidence**: GET vault documents and rule registry by bound IDs, version and service dates, 10s timeout; one read retry then unavailable. Reject cross-subject records. Strip hidden text, scripts and invisible instructions; low OCR confidence or conflicting layers route to staff.
- **C3 Explain evidence**: No authority. Context maximum 8000 tokens; longer files route to staff rather than silent truncation. Structured output validation and a single retry; 30s each; no tools or memory.
- **C4 Validate policy and calculate**: Match every quote byte-for-byte after fixed whitespace normalization to admitted document/page/clause; validate policy dates in code. Parse bill quantities into integer minor units via deterministic schema; ambiguous digit or currency needs staff. No model amount accepted. Proposed engine: rule pack explicitly sets admission/service-date applicability, room-day counting, rounding, room-only vs proportional-linked-charge reduction, exclusions, deductibles, copay order and remaining cover. Each must be certified H2. Synthetic rule RV1: 2 counted days, room cap 500000 minor/day; room charge 1200000 => eligible room 1000000. Other eligible charge 2000000 stays whole for room-only rule => 3000000 total. RV2 proportional rule applies 5/6 only to explicitly linked 1800000, unlinked 200000 => 1000000+1500000+200000=2700000. Round each affected line half-up to minor unit; duplicate lines flagged, not dropped silently. Unknown proration, date gaps, endorsements or accumulator freshness => review with no authoritative total. No deductible/copay assumed absent: explicit zero rules required. First release supports only explicit zero deductible and zero copay; nonzero values, mixed currency or service dates spanning rule periods route review. Apply min(calculated eligible, fresh remainingCoverMinor); remaining cover comes from an authoritative accumulator version, not document text. This recommendation reserves no benefits; fresh recheck by insurer is mandatory before settlement. Exclude clinical necessity, fraud and medically coded exclusion judgement from automated calculation; route these to staff. Monetary inputs must be nonnegative signed-64-bit integers in one trusted currency. Reject negative/overflow values. Day count must match certified admission/discharge convention; do not use a model date. Missing accumulator version or staleness => review.
- **C5 Own human review**: Queue: claims-review; proposed lead H4, ack four elapsed hours, resolve 24 elapsed hours, escalate unacked at four to backup supervisor. Rejections/unknown rules never leave as automated final decisions. Resolution may record manual-review-required; adding rules requires separately certified new registry version. Own W11 unresolved operation tickets across caller expiry and denied security outcomes without revealing medical data. For signed resolution, authenticate examiner identity/role, exact case version and immutable review event before constructing review payload; reject changed snapshot, stale or unsigned events.
- **C6 Show waiting status**: Render read-only case status through authenticated dashboard pull; no email or external message. One-hour timer does not renew the 24h deadline. UI unavailable raises operations alert and keeps queue ownership.
- **C7 Save staff-facing case**: PUT /staff-cases/{claim}/operations/{operationId}; authority case.write only, no payment permission. Verify authenticated scope, fresh role, current claim and rule version, full immutable payload SHA256 from stored bytes, status enum and expectedVersion at API. Unique tenant/claim/submissionRevision prevents exact duplicates; semantic duplicate candidates require human linking, never auto-merge. CAS makes only one concurrent revision succeed. 10s timeout; no blind write retry. Narrative cannot change numeric fields. Internal serialization is UTF-8 JSON with sorted keys, compact separators and no nonfinite numbers; SHA256 stored bytes, not a reserialized model object. Store immutable bytes before authorizing the save. Any payload change requires new operation authorization. Ledger business identity is tenant/claim/submissionRevision. Nonmedical security telemetry remains separate from claim record writes.
- **C8 Reconcile save**: GET operation record under service-owned read-only reconciliation scope, independent of expired caller session. Three reads at 10s with server backoff 5/20/60s; require payload bytes match and deduplicate callbacks by eventId. Retry once only after confirmed_absent plus fresh auth/CAS; otherwise owned unresolved. Cancellation during ambiguity freezes follow-up; finish readback, no compensation or new intent.
- **C9 Guard record proof**: Validate record IDs, subject, operation, full stored bytes and version through readback before user sees saved. Invalid/missing proof retains owner and unresolved status.

## Framework

- **F1 Durable code state machine**: Select on durable waits, audit, replay and retry control. Database workflow is enough for eleven nodes; a workflow engine is an unverified candidate if timer/replay load grows.
- **F2 Separate records and retrieval**: Documents establish evidence; insurer registry establishes policy. Tenant-keyed reads, row isolation, encryption, secrets vault, PHI-free telemetry; deletion/hold and region blocked on H3.
- **F3 Narrow case writer**: One case revision blast radius; fresh RBAC/CAS, full-payload binding, isolated reconciliation reader. Service owns unresolved operations after session expiry.
- **F4 Provider-neutral private deployment**: Compare candidates on region, access boundaries, durable timers, deletion evidence and operating burden with identical tests; no current vendor claims.

## Models

- **M1 One bounded explanation call**: W3 only: expected 0.8*(1+0.10)=0.88 calls/submission; worst 2. USD/call=(8000*2+1000*8)/1e6=0.024. Expected 0.02112/case, worst 0.048; 10000/month => USD211.20 model tokens. Excludes OCR, storage, human work, network, taxes and extra-large files, which route to staff. Standard tier chosen for mixed document reasoning; small tier requires equal evidence accuracy; reasoning tier only if held-out complex-clause gains justify latency. Two 30s calls maximum; unavailable -> staff.

## Rollout

- **R1 Measure examiner time and decision quality**: Measure 2 weeks of eligible case active handling minutes (median per resolved case), draft correctness exact case proportion, and no-PHI breach counts; report strata and unresolved cases separately. H6 target 20% lower median time is hypothesis. Source accuracy affects rework; timers affect delay; cost is a separate guardrail.
- **R2 Shadow then assist**: Shadow entry H1-H4 verified and EV21 synthetic/adapter gate. Exit after two representative weeks: every safety assertion passes, experts inspect all policy/date/room-rule strata and disagreement reasons. Assist entry shadow exit + staffed queue. Assist exit after four weeks: zero safety events, acceptable examiner feedback and full audit joins. Remain assist-only; no automatic claim settlement pilot or scale in this scope.
- **R3 Test handling-time benefit**: Cluster-randomize examiner teams to assistant/control; stratify by plan, room cap and document type. Keep teams in one arm to reduce contamination. Both arms retain same human approval. Four-week observation minimum after baseline; analytics calculates continuous-time variance, chosen minimum detectable effect and 80% power/5% significance with team ICC/design effect; extend duration if underpowered. Report error proportions separately with exact intervals; no averaging away safety.
- **R4 Own rollout and unresolved cases**: Director signs expansion only after EV21 and H1-H4 evidence; privacy/rules/security owners can veto safety. Operations owns unresolved save tickets across caller expiry, reviews every four hours, escalates to incident lead at deadline. Human approves any future settlement scope as a separate design.
