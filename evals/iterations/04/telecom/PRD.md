# Telecom complaint and service credit assistant

> NEEDS-WORK | DESIGN ONLY. independent-agent; round 1.

## Original brief

Design a complaint assistant for a telecom provider. Customers report network outages, poor speed and billing errors. The assistant should investigate using account, billing and service records, explain findings and resolve eligible service credits. Larger or unclear refunds need a human supervisor and may wait up to 48 hours. Keep customers informed, avoid duplicate refunds and use only the right customer's active service connection. Propose any missing policies as assumptions, not real company rules.


## Context

- **CTX1 Decision and boundary**: Customers and support staff investigate outages, slow speed and billing errors. Automatic action is a service-ledger credit for a confirmed outage under H1. Speed, billing errors, large or unclear amounts go to a supervisor. Cash payouts, plan changes, network repair and historical inactive services are excluded. Start: authenticated complaint or provisional intake. End: evidence-backed explanation plus posted credit, no-credit finding, cancelled case, or an owned unresolved case with next update time.
- **CTX2 Authoritative evidence**: Identity gateway supplies tenantId/customerId and scoped service choices. Account registry supplies active connection and version. Billing ledger supplies original recurring charge, cycle boundaries and already credited amount. Service telemetry supplies outage IDs, verified intervals, region and freshness. Published policy registry supplies signed effective version. Missing or contradictory evidence yields provisional human investigation; it does not prove no outage. Customer text may locate evidence but cannot override a record.
- **CTX3 Delivery and success**: Reduce elapsed resolution hours without wrong-customer or duplicate credit. Business baseline unknown: collect two weeks before experiment. All control fixtures are synthetic specifications, not executed business tests. Adapter support, policy authority, queue staffing and privacy sign-off are launch blockers H1–H4,H6. All times are UTC instants and elapsed hours, independent of browser session.

## Components

- **C1 Identity and scoped intake**: Bind IDs from gateway and account registry, never from text. Refuse any mismatched/inactive selection before private reads. Case persisted transactionally with reason and owner; provisional cases contain no private billing data. GET account timeout 5s, one read retry, then NEED_INFO.
- **C2 Scoped evidence reader**: Read only exact tenant/customer/active connection. Typed evidence includes observedAt, resourceVersion, allowed fields, source IDs. Timeout 5s per source, one read retry; missing, stale (>5 minutes for active state), conflicting or unavailable fields route to supervisor. Interval union clips to billing-cycle UTC boundaries; never double-count overlapping outages.
- **C3 Evidence-grounded explanation**: One call, deadline 12s. May explain service/billing evidence, cannot supply amount, dates or IDs for writes. Code verifies citations against admitted source fields; invalid/unavailable output uses fixed template and human route. Customer and retrieved text are untrusted; no tool execution and no hidden HTML/document layer is admitted.
- **C4 Rule and action validation**: P1 in H1 computes integer minor-unit credit: floor(chargeMinor × union outageMinutes / cycleMinutes), then compare raw calculated amount to the 50000 automatic cap. Above cap routes HUMAN. Otherwise use min(calculated,chargeMinor-alreadyCreditedMinor). Requires positive charge/cycle, nonnegative already credited, verified outage minutes 0..cycle length and valid policy. Zero amount is NO_CREDIT. Above automatic cap routes HUMAN before capping, never silently pays a partial amount. Supervisor exception command may specify positive integer amount up to remaining original charge; unsupported cash refunds receive owned manual disposition. Approval binds complete command bytes: tenant/customer/connection, originalChargeId, cycle, outage IDs, amountMinor,currency,reason,allocation,policyVersion,accountVersion,operationId,expiresAt. Any changed field needs new decision. Fresh auth/account/policy/ledger rechecks at writer, including expiry and revocation; missing support BLOCKED.
- **C5 Durable supervisor queue**: Internal workflow store owns queue assignment and durable events across session expiry. Proposed owner Support supervisor; independent billing approver for exceptions above P1. ACK within 2 elapsed hours; reminder 24h, escalation 48h to duty manager with owned unresolved case. Server timestamps, deduplicated events, compare-and-swap caseVersion. Approval token is actor-signed, single-use, exact command, 15-minute validity; writer separately verifies authority. Cancel before submit suppresses action; cancellation after uncertain submit freezes conflicting commands until lookup. Missing evidence permits owned provisional investigation.
- **C6 Customer status delivery**: Send only minimized status; template says pending, approved-not-posted, credited with proof, overdue or unresolved. Never says refunded on approval alone. 5s timeout; no direct retry after uncertain send. Query by noticeKey; once confirmed absent retry once under same key. Failed channel remains visible in-app and duty manager owns delivery issue. No private account data on unauthenticated channel.
- **C7 Service-ledger credit writer**: POST credit to exact original charge. Fetch immutable command and verify bytes/approval binding; currency INR, amountMinor integer >0, remaining charge bound, active service, same tenant/customer, current versions, actor authority, expiry and cancellation. Atomic unique semantic key tenant/customer/connection/incident-set/cycle/credit-type blocks cross-case duplicate; operationId deduplicates retries. Concurrent overlapping-interval complaints lock billing-cycle adjustment budget and correlate semantic matches with human before any second operation. POSTED requires durable postingId, amount/currency/originalChargeId and versions matching command. REJECTED has reason and no effect; 5s timeout/unknown require lookup. No generic retry.
- **C8 Read-only reconciliation**: Lookup uses service credential scoped to original tenant and operation, survives user logout. Deduplicate callbacks by eventId+operationId and reject mismatched proof. Poll at 1m,5m,30m,2h; then unresolved with duty manager and no automatic future write. ABSENT_FINAL must prove operation cannot later commit before one resubmission under original key and fresh checks. Session expiry does not revoke lookup; revocation blocks any new action. Cancellation after COMMITTED records posted/cancellation requested; reversal requires separately authorized new case outside first-release scope.
- **C9 Record proof and close**: Credit completion requires readback matching all command fields; all other dispositions require persisted case reason/owner/version. Unknown is not completed refund and retains operation lock. Missing/mismatched proof becomes owned unresolved status; no success assertion. Code templates verify LLM explanation citations and all amounts before display. Explicit proof projection: CREDITED requires a matching ledger posting from C7/C8; C1 case IDs can never prove a credit. Other states require a fresh scoped durable case record with reason, owner and version. Missing/empty/wrong-source ID yields MISSING, never VERIFIED.

## Framework

- **F1 Durable code workflow**: Transactional case state machine with bounded explanation call; SQL outbox/event log and supervised queue. Rejected: ['Single open-ended tool agent; synchronous chat-only chain.']
- **F2 Action scopes**: Separate read, credit and notice service principals; writer fetches exact immutable command and rechecks authority. Rejected: ['Shared administrator token.']
- **F3 Authoritative storage**: Relational case/operation ledger and immutable evidence snapshots; no vector store is authoritative. Rejected: ['Put all billing/account state in retrieval index.']

## Models

- **M1 One bounded explanation call**: Choose standard tier for conflicting service/billing evidence explanation. Small tier rejected until it passes same evidence/abstention strata; reasoning tier adds latency without write authority. W3 exactly once per identity-valid case; read failure skips it. H5 upper expected calls 0.9/case (assumes all valid reads succeed); worst 1/case, no retries. At 2400/450 tokens and illustrative USD 1/4 per million, call cost = .0024+.0018=.0042; upper expected .00378/case and USD 37.80/10000 monthly cases; worst .0042/case. Analyze intent-to-treat; report safety separately and do not average away breaches.
